- KPI values around 32px bold
- card labels ~12/18 medium
- table/supporting text mostly 11–13px

- white primary surfaces

- 1200px rail

## A6-specific
- denser information hierarchy than A1/A2/A3
- KPI cards may use large numeric emphasis without large page hero
- main composition is summary -> controls/template -> table
- flat table/list rows are preferred over converting every record into a card
- operational density is acceptable and expected
